About me

Hi, I’m Andrea, but most people call me RIRI.

I design digital products that solve real problems, feel good to use and make everyday life a little easier.

I'm a product designer in Abuja, currently a UI/UX Designer at Seismic Consulting. Before design, I studied Biotechnology at Nile University of Nigeria and worked in a diagnostic lab, where I saw first-hand how people experience healthcare.

That background shapes how I work: research first, careful with evidence, and always thinking about the person on the other side of the screen. It's also why I care so much about health tech, women's health and mental wellbeing.

Many of my projects start with something I love. Perfume bottles became Elixir, beautifully made furniture became Oakwood, and my interest in mental health became Soulgarden.
